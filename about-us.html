Our Story

Built on one belief:
execution is everything.

Event 1O1 was founded in 2019 by Nikhil Sharma — a DJ turned event producer who understood that the biggest gap in Ahmedabad's event industry wasn't talent, it was coordination and ownership.

After years of performing at weddings and watching clients struggle to manage 8 different vendors — DJ, sound, stage, lighting, SFX, anchor, décor, logistics — Nikhil built Event 1O1 to be the single point of accountability for the entire production.

Today, Event 1O1 is Ahmedabad's most trusted name for weddings, corporate events, festive celebrations, and brand activations — serving clients across Gujarat and India with a 5-star Google reputation and zero-compromise execution.
