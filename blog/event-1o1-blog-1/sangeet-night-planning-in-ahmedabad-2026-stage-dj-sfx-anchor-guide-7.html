What Makes a Great Sangeet Night in Ahmedabad

A sangeet night is not just a music event — it is a performance stage for your family. The energy of the evening depends on three things: a DJ who reads the crowd, a stage that looks spectacular on camera, and lighting that creates the right mood from the first song to the last dance.

Stage Setup for Sangeet — What You Need

A standard sangeet stage includes a 20x25 ft to 25x30 ft elevated platform, backdrop with LED panel, and couple sofa setup. For premium sangeet nights, a box truss structure with moving head lights transforms even a basic banquet hall.

  • Stage platform — 2 to 2.5 ft height, carpeted, with stairs on both sides
  • LED backdrop — Customised with couple name and design
  • Backdrop + Sofa — For couple seating during performances
  • Box Truss or Goal Post Truss — For lighting rig around the hall
  • Entry passage — 30 ft illuminated passage for grand couple entry

Stage setup cost in Ahmedabad for sangeet: Rs.35,000 to Rs.1,50,000 depending on size, truss, and LED elements.

DJ and Sound System for Sangeet

For a 200-400 guest sangeet in Ahmedabad, you need minimum 2 pair professional speakers, subwoofers, and stage monitors. A basic DJ setup starts at Rs.10,000 — a full professional line array system starts at Rs.35,000 to Rs.60,000.

SFX — Special Effects for Sangeet Night

The most popular SFX for sangeet in Ahmedabad 2026:

  • CO2 Jets — Burst of fog at peak moments, Rs.3,000 per piece
  • Sparkular SFX — Cold spark fountains, completely indoor-safe, Rs.3,000 per piece
  • Cold Pyro — Ground-level fire effects, safe for indoor use
  • Flower Shower — Rose petals from above during couple entry
  • Bubble Machine — Aesthetic effect for slow songs and couple moments

A standard SFX package — 2 Sparkular, 2 CO2, Cold Pyro x6 — costs approximately Rs.15,000 to Rs.20,000.

Anchor for Sangeet Night

A bilingual anchor in Hindi and Gujarati is essential for sangeet nights in Ahmedabad. A professional sangeet anchor charges Rs.12,000 to Rs.25,000 depending on experience and programme length.

Sangeet Night Budget — Ahmedabad 2026

  • Budget setup (100-200 guests): Rs.50,000 to Rs.80,000
  • Standard setup (200-400 guests): Rs.1,00,000 to Rs.1,80,000
  • Premium setup (400+ guests, box truss, full LED, SFX): Rs.2,00,000 to Rs.4,00,000

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a sangeet night cost in Ahmedabad?

A complete sangeet production costs Rs.50,000 to Rs.4,00,000 depending on guest count, venue size, stage setup, and SFX requirements.

Do I need a separate DJ for sangeet and reception?

Not necessarily. Many couples book the same DJ for all functions — sangeet, varmaala, and reception — which saves cost and ensures consistency. Event 1O1 provides package pricing for multi-function bookings.

What SFX is best for sangeet night?

The most popular combination is Sparkular SFX + CO2 Jets + Flower Shower. This gives visual impact at key moments — couple entry, family performance finales, and peak dance floor segments.

Is a sangeet anchor necessary?

For sangeet nights with family performances, an anchor is strongly recommended. A professional anchor handles programme flow, guest engagement, and timeline management seamlessly.
