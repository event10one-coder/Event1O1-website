Privacy Policy

Event 1O1 — Ahmedabad  |  Last updated: August 2026

Event 1O1 is a full-service event management company in Ahmedabad, Gujarat. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use and protect your personal information when you visit www.event1o1.com or contact us for wedding, corporate or festive event management services.

1

Information We Collect

  • Contact details: Name, phone number, email address when you submit an event enquiry
  • Event details: Wedding date, event type, venue, guest count, and budget shared during consultation
  • Usage data: Pages visited, time spent, browser type — collected via Google Analytics 4 and GTM
2

How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to wedding, corporate and festive event enquiries
  • To send event quotes, updates and follow-ups
  • To run Google Ads and Meta advertising campaigns
  • For GST invoicing and legal compliance under Indian law
3

Cookies and Tracking

Our website uses Google Analytics 4 (GA4), Google Tag Manager, Google Ads conversion tracking, and Meta Pixel for website analytics and advertising optimization. You may disable cookies in your browser settings at any time.
4

Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal data. We share information only with trusted event vendors as needed to deliver your event — and with Google and Meta for advertising purposes, governed by their respective privacy policies.
5

Data Retention

We retain your personal information as long as necessary to deliver services and comply with Indian legal obligations under the Information Technology Act, 2000.
6

Your Rights

You have the right to request access, correction, or deletion of your personal data at any time. Contact us at contact@event1o1.com or call +91 95379 68007.
7

Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your data against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.
