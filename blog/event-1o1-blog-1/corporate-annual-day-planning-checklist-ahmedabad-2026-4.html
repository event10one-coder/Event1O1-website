Corporate Annual Day Planning Checklist Ahmedabad 2026

Event 1O1·Aug 2026
Home›Blog›Corporate Annual Day Planning Checklist Ahmedabad 2026

Annual Day is the most visible event a company produces all year. It is the one event your employees talk about for months after — positively or negatively. A well-executed Annual Day builds culture, celebrates achievement, and reinforces brand identity. A poorly planned one communicates that the company does not value its people.

This guide is for HR managers, admin heads, and executive assistants in Ahmedabad and Gujarat who are responsible for planning their company's Annual Day. It covers the full planning timeline, vendor checklist, budget planning, and the questions you need to ask every vendor before signing a contract.

What Is Corporate Annual Day?

Corporate Annual Day (also called Annual Function, Staff Day, or Founders Day) is a company's internal celebration — typically held once a year — where the organisation recognises employee achievements, presents awards, hosts entertainment, and reinforces its culture and values.

Common Annual Day formats include:

  • Awards ceremony + dinner — formal recognition event with entertainment
  • Theme-based party — costume theme, decade theme, or cultural theme with games and performances
  • Town hall + celebration — MD/CEO address followed by recognition and entertainment
  • Offsite annual retreat — team outing at a resort with structured activities and a gala night

The 12-Week Planning Timeline

Start planning 12 weeks (3 months) before your event date. Most Annual Day events in Ahmedabad happen between October and February — meaning planning should begin in July–August for a December event.

12 Weeks Before — Define the Brief

  • Lock the event date and venue
  • Get budget approval from management
  • Decide the event format (awards + dinner, theme party, offsite, etc.)
  • Define expected attendance (employees only, or with family?)
  • Identify who will give the MD/CEO address and approximate duration

10 Weeks Before — Vendor Shortlisting

  • Issue RFQ to at least 3 event management companies
  • Review vendor portfolios — look specifically for corporate Annual Day experience
  • Check if the vendor is GEM-registered (mandatory for PSUs and government companies)
  • Shortlist and schedule site visits with top 2 vendors

8 Weeks Before — Confirm Vendors and Awards

  • Sign contract with your event management company
  • Pay advance (typically 40–50% of total cost)
  • Finalise award categories and recipients
  • Confirm entertainment — live performers, DJ, games
  • Brief on branding requirements (logo placement, backdrop design, AV content)

6 Weeks Before — Design and Content

  • Approve stage backdrop design and branding visuals
  • Share award winner names and designations for certificates/trophies
  • Finalise the programme runsheet (minute-by-minute schedule)
  • Confirm catering menu and special dietary requirements
  • Send invitations / internal communication to employees

4 Weeks Before — Logistics Lock

  • Confirm transport arrangements
  • Finalise table seating plan (if formal dinner format)
  • Confirm photographer and videographer brief
  • Brief all internal stakeholders — department heads, MC, award presenters

2 Weeks Before — Final Checks

  • Venue walkthrough with event team
  • Rehearsal for award ceremony sequence
  • Confirm all trophies/certificates are ready
  • Send reminder communication to all employees

Event Day — Go-Live Protocol

  • Event team arrives 4–5 hours before guests for setup
  • AV and sound check with all presenters
  • Full rehearsal for MD address and award presentation
  • Registration desk operational 30 minutes before doors open

The Complete Annual Day Vendor Checklist

Production and AV

  • Stage and backdrop (with company branding)
  • LED screen (for presentations, video montages, live feed)
  • Sound system (line array for halls above 300 pax)
  • Lighting (stage lighting, audience wash, moving heads)
  • Projector + laptop for presentations
  • Wireless microphones (minimum 4 — MD, anchor, award presenters, roving)
  • Generator backup

Entertainment

  • Professional anchor / emcee (bilingual — English + Hindi/Gujarati)
  • Live performer (singer, comedian, band) — optional
  • DJ for post-awards dinner/party
  • Games host (if team-building activities are included)

Décor and Branding

  • Stage backdrop with company name and theme
  • Entry gate / branded welcome arch
  • Standees and cutouts (award categories display)
  • Step-and-repeat banner for photo opportunities

Awards and Recognition

  • Trophies / plaques (order minimum 4 weeks in advance)
  • Framed certificates
  • Award citation cards (read out during ceremony)
  • Momento for long-service awardees

Photography and Video

  • Event photographer
  • Videographer + drone (if outdoor / large venue)
  • Photo booth (employees share on social media)
  • Year-in-review video compilation (brief 4 weeks before)

Choosing the Right Event Vendor — 5 Questions to Ask

1. Have you managed Annual Day events for companies in our industry?
Manufacturing and IT Annual Days are completely different in tone and format. Ask for industry-specific references.

2. Can you provide a detailed programme runsheet before we sign the contract?
A professional vendor should be able to draft a preliminary runsheet based on your brief.

3. What is your backup plan if the main anchor cancels on the day?
It happens. What is their contingency?

4. Do you handle award printing and trophies, or is that separate?
Many companies get caught out with this. Confirm in writing what is and is not included.

5. Are you GEM-registered?
If your company is a PSU or government entity, this is non-negotiable. Event 1O1 is GEM-registered under the Event Management category.

Mistakes HR Managers Make Every Year

Starting too late — Annual Day planning that begins 4 weeks before the event results in compromises on venue, vendor quality, and awards. 12 weeks is the minimum.

Leaving the programme to the vendor — The event company produces the event. You own the programme. Define every minute of the schedule internally before briefing the vendor.

No single point of contact on the company side — Multiple departments giving conflicting instructions on event day causes confusion. Designate one decision-maker.

Underestimating setup time — For events above 300 people with full stage, AV, and décor, allow minimum 6–8 hours for setup.

Forgetting the year-in-review video — This is always the emotional highlight of any Annual Day. Brief your team at least 4 weeks in advance.
