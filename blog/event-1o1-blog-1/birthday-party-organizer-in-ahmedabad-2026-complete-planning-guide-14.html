Planning a birthday party in Ahmedabad? Whether intimate gathering of 20 friends or grand celebration with 500+ guests — this guide covers everything: decoration themes, DJ, SFX, anchor, venue selection, and what it costs in 2026.

At Event 1O1, we have organized hundreds of birthday parties across Ahmedabad. Here is everything you need to know before booking.

Popular Birthday Party Themes in Ahmedabad 2026

  • Bollywood Night — Classic Indian cinema theme with retro decor and Bollywood music
  • Dark and Gold Luxury — Premium black and gold aesthetic — popular for milestone birthdays
  • Royal/Maharaja Theme — Gold and maroon decor, throne setup, traditional elements
  • Retro 80s/90s Theme — Neon colors, vintage elements, nostalgia-driven music
  • Minimal Modern — Clean pastel tones, balloon installations, Instagram-worthy setup
  • Tropical/Destination Theme — Beach, floral, outdoor aesthetic for farmhouse parties

Birthday Party Decoration in Ahmedabad

Key decoration elements for a birthday event in Ahmedabad:

  • Personalized backdrop — Name and age — printed or LED
  • Balloon installations — Walls, arches, ceiling fills, organic arrangements
  • Stage or focal point — Cake table, throne setup, or main stage
  • Entry decoration — Welcome arch, name gate, photo opportunity setup
  • Lighting — LED fairy lights, uplighting, neon signs, gobo lights

Birthday party decoration cost in Ahmedabad: Rs.15,000 to Rs.1,50,000+ depending on venue size and theme complexity.

DJ and Music for Birthday Party Ahmedabad

A birthday party DJ needs to match guest demographics — Bollywood hits for older crowds, EDM and Punjabi pop for younger audiences, or a mix for multi-generational parties. For 50-200 guests, a standard DJ setup costs Rs.8,000 to Rs.20,000.

SFX for Birthday Party Ahmedabad

  • Sparkular SFX — Cold spark fountains for cake entry — indoor-safe, visually stunning
  • CO2 Jets — Burst of fog at peak dance moments
  • Flower Shower — Rose petals from above during birthday entry
  • Confetti Cannon — Paper confetti blast at cake cutting moment

Basic SFX package (2 Sparkular + Confetti Cannon): Rs.8,000 to Rs.12,000.

Birthday Party Cost in Ahmedabad 2026

  • Small (20-50 guests): Rs.15,000 to Rs.40,000
  • Medium (50-150 guests): Rs.40,000 to Rs.1,00,000
  • Large (150-300 guests): Rs.1,00,000 to Rs.2,50,000
  • Grand (300+ guests): Rs.2,50,000 and above

Milestone Birthday Parties

1st Birthday: Soft pastel themes, balloon heaven setups, baby-friendly decor. 25th Birthday: Silver jubilee theme, cocktail-style setup. 50th Birthday: Golden anniversary style with family-focused cultural elements.
