About Event 1O1

Ahmedabad's full-service event production company.

Event 1O1 is Ahmedabad's premier full-service event management and production company, founded by Nikhil Sharma with 7+ years of live event production experience and 500+ successful events across Gujarat and India.

We specialise in weddings, corporate Annual Days, brand activations, NYE celebrations and destination weddings. Our services include professional DJ sound systems, LED stage setup, CO2 gun effects, stage SFX, fireworks, Baraat on Wheel, Punjabi Dholi, anchor, visual branding, décor coordination, vintage cars, and complete end-to-end event management.

From intimate 50-guest sangeet nights to grand 2,000-guest receptions — we have produced it all. One call — everything covered.

Our Process

How we work

From your first call to the last song of the night — here's how Event 1O1 delivers flawless events every time.

1
Free Consultation

We understand your vision, event type, guest count, venue and budget. No commitment required. Call or WhatsApp +91 95379 68007.

2
Custom Proposal

Within 24 hours we share a detailed event plan, vendor list and transparent pricing. What you see is what you pay — no hidden charges.

3
Vendor & Production Tie-Up

We lock in the best vendors, book equipment and confirm all production elements 2 weeks before your event. Zero last-minute surprises.

4
Day-Of Execution

Our team arrives 3 hours before your event. Full setup, sound check, lighting test and rehearsal done before the first guest arrives.

5
Smooth Pack-Up

After your event ends, our crew handles complete breakdown and exit. Venue left spotless. No extra charges. No mess.
